FocusCam

A PDF reader with an AI study buddy built in. It learns how you look when you're focused and nudges you the moment you drift.

Continue reading

  • PDFs and books you study will appear here, so you can pick up where you stopped.

How it works

  1. Neural nets watch your face (478 points + 52 expressions), eyes, head angle and both hands, about 15 times a second. A third model spots phones.
  2. Your personal AI model combines that with how you read (scrolling, page turns, taps) and learns what your focus looks like. Anything unusual for you gets flagged.
  3. Write on your PDFs with Apple Pencil: pen, highlighter and eraser. Notes are saved with each PDF.
  4. You teach it. Every alert asks "Did you drift?" Your answer retrains it, and it's saved on this iPad.

🔒 Everything runs on this iPad. No video or PDF is uploaded anywhere.

📐 Use a stand so the front camera sees your face. If you solve on paper, calibration also learns your "looking at notebook" pose.
